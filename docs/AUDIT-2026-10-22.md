## Defects that survived the previous pass

1. **The Research entry point was invisible.** `RESEARCH` was `--ax-ink`
   (near-white) on the light header bar: **1.02:1**. It shipped that way, and the
   "after" screenshot offered as proof shows the blank cell. `WHY` was 4.33:1
   (AA needs 4.5). The BANGKOK line, `FIND`, the search placeholder and `PLAYS`
   were 3.93:1 — the dark ground's dim-ink token used on a paper ground. The last
   audit measured boxes and never measured contrast.
2. **Header and controls sized by declaration, not content.** `min-height: 76px`
   (64px on a phone) with every control stretched to fill it. Now derived from
   the 44px search field: **45px** on both viewports (chrome 7.6–8.4% → 5.0–5.3%),
   tool row 32px label-plus-padding.
3. **Two weak detectors.** `countClaims` caught 3 of 8 planted stale claims
   (`80`, `eighty`, `ninety-nine` — exactly the phrasings that had already
   happened) and silently *skipped* anything it recognised but could not
   resolve. `registryAgreement` missed double-quoted orphan keys in two of five
   registries (a hand-rolled regex parser). Both rebuilt; probes now 10/10 and
   7/7.
4. **Controls under the 24px WCAG floor.** The `READ THE PAPERS` and
   `OPEN-SOURCE CREDITS` disclosure triggers were 14px tall: the honesty
   section's own door was the hardest thing on the page to hit.
5. **Boxes padded to 44px "to be tap targets".** Twelve citation links at 52px
   pitch (a 624px list round 13px of type), footer credits, the research tabs on
   a phone (44px), room tabs with a declared 84px floor, a 64px fixed PLAY bar
   round one word, and a stray `min-height: 52px` override on Today's Five.
   §11.12 says dense text controls are 32px, never padded to 44.
6. **The instrument was never committed.** The measuring scripts lived in
   `/tmp`; the audit left no guard, so the next agent re-introduces all of this.
   → `scripts/geometry-audit.mjs`, wired into `scripts/deploy.sh`.
7. **Migration ledger drift** — documented and guarded, not repaired. See
   `migrations/README.md`.

## Named for the next audit (found, not fixed)

These are real and were left alone on purpose — they are palette decisions, not
spatial ones, and a sweep that can't see photo-backed panels would guess.

- **White text on amber is 2.15:1.** 9px labels on the cartridge code chips
  (`NBK`), the room-tab discs, the `PLAY` button label, the difficulty stickers.
  §11.10 says this fails. Dark paper ink on amber is 9:1.
- **`--ax-ink-3` (`#6b7785`) is 3.9:1 on the dark panels** (`TEEN+` 3.44,
  `3–5 min · TRAIN` 3.91, the room counts) and 3.93:1 on the paper header. One
  value cannot serve both grounds; the header now redefines it locally, the dark
  side has not been decided.
- **`.briefing-play:hover` is `#8e2924`** — a dark red, a second hue (§14 r1).
- **`scripts/visual-audit.sh` forks ~500 processes** (`grep` per match) and took
  2 minutes under load; a single Node pass would take well under a second.
- **The machine was at load average 380** (several agent apps and a dozen dev
  servers at once). That, not the repo, is why the audits were slow, and why the
  geometry audit waits up to 120s for Chrome.
- **`context.md` carries an uncommitted `gamification-motion` entry** written by
  another session. Left untouched and uncommitted.

## Limits of this audit

R5 judges the header only: a computed-style audit cannot see a photo or
pseudo-element behind text, and a wrong guess there is how the last detector
produced 55 findings of which none were real. Thresholds are a ratchet at the
current good state. The live smoke was not re-run; parity was checked, and the
pages were measured locally in Chrome at 390 and 1440.
